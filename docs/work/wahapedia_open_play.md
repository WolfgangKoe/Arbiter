## Overview

Open Play (Open Hostility Mission Pack) is the casual, narrative-friendly format. There are no strict army composition requirements or points limits. Armies can be any size and need not be equal. Power Level is used as an optional balancing guide. Three missions cover different power imbalance scenarios.

---

## Battle Size Reference (Power Level Based)

| Battle Size   | Combined Power Level | Expected Duration |
|---------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Combat Patrol | ~50                 | Up to 1 hour      |
| Incursion     | ~100                | Up to 2 hours     |
| Strike Force  | ~200                | Up to 3 hours     |
| Onslaught     | ~300                | Up to 4 hours     |

Power Level = sum of all unit Power Ratings in the army. Each unit's Power Rating appears on its datasheet; it increases when adding models or certain equipment options.

## The Open Play Sequence (12 Steps)

### STEP 1 — MUSTER ARMIES
Players each select models from their collection. No size requirement or points limit applies; armies need not be equal in size. 

**Warlord Selection:**
- Each player selects one model as their Warlord; that model gains the WARLORD keyword.
- If the Warlord has the CHARACTER keyword, the player chooses a Warlord Trait.
- All Warlords may take the "Inspiring Leader" trait: "Add 1 to the Leadership characteristic of friendly units while they are within 6" of this WARLORD."

### STEP 2 — DETERMINE MISSION
Players either agree on a mission or roll randomly using a D6 table based on the relative Power Levels of the two armies:

| D6 Roll | Mission            | When Used                                              |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| 1–2     | Annihilation       | Armies of roughly similar Power Level                  |
| 3–4     | Hold at All Costs | One army is 1/3 greater to less than twice the other's Power Level |
| 5–6     | Death or Glory    | One army is at least twice the other's Power Level     |

### STEP 3 — READ MISSION BRIEFING
Players familiarize themselves with the mission's objectives and any special rules.

### STEP 4 — CREATE THE BATTLEFIELD
Set up terrain on a rectangular battlefield. Minimum sizes by battle category:

| Battle Category         | Minimum Battlefield Size |
|------------------------|-------------------------|
| Combat Patrol/Incursion| 44" × 30"               |
| Strike Force           | 44" × 60"               |
| Onslaught              | 44" × 90"               |

### STEP 5 — DETERMINE ATTACKER AND DEFENDER
If Power Levels differ, the player with the higher Power Rating becomes the Attacker. If equal, players roll off; the winner decides their role.

### STEP 6 — DECLARE RESERVES AND TRANSPORTS
Players secretly note which units will start off-battlefield or embarked in TRANSPORT models, then simultaneously reveal selections.

### STEP 7 — DEPLOY ARMIES
Players alternate deploying units one at a time, starting with the Attacker. Units must be set up wholly within their deployment zone. If one player finishes first, the opponent deploys all remaining units.

### STEP 8 — DETERMINE FIRST TURN
Players roll off unless the mission states otherwise. The winner decides who takes the first turn.

### STEP 9 — RESOLVE PRE-BATTLE ABILITIES
Players alternate resolving pre-battle abilities, starting with the player taking the first turn.

### STEP 10 — BEGIN THE BATTLE
Resolve successive battle rounds.

### STEP 11 — END THE BATTLE
The battle ends when one army is completely destroyed or after the fifth battle round completes — whichever comes first.

### STEP 12 — DETERMINE VICTOR
Players check mission objectives to identify the winner.

## Mission Rules

### Mission 1: ANNIHILATION (D6: 1–2)
*Designed for armies of roughly similar Power Level.*

**No special rules.**

**Victory Conditions:**
- If one army is completely destroyed, the opposing player wins automatically.
- Otherwise, the player with the most victory points wins (tie = draw).

**Victory Points Scoring:**
- *Slay the Warlord:* "A player scores 1 victory point if the enemy WARLORD is destroyed at the end of the battle."
- *Purge the Enemy:* "At the end of the battle, each player adds up the Power Rating of all enemy units that were destroyed during the battle and divides the total by 10 (rounding up) — this is the number of victory points that player scores."

---

### Mission 2: HOLD AT ALL COSTS (D6: 3–4)
*Designed for battles where one army's Power Level is between 1/3 greater and less than twice the other's.*

**Special Rule — Dug-in Positions:**
"In the first battle round, the Defender adds 1 to all saving throws taken for their models that are wholly within their own deployment zone (invulnerable saves are unaffected)."

**Victory Conditions:**
- If one army is completely destroyed, the opposing player wins.
- Otherwise, the player whose model is closest to the battlefield center wins (tie = draw).
- This mission does NOT use standard victory point totals — proximity to center is the tiebreaker.

---

### Mission 3: DEATH OR GLORY (D6: 5–6)
*Designed for battles where one army's Power Level is at least twice the other's.*

**Special Rule — No Retreat:**
"In this mission, the Defender automatically passes all Morale tests taken for their units (no dice are rolled)."

**First Turn:** The Defender decides who takes the first turn (not a roll-off).

**Victory Conditions:**
- If the Defender's army is destroyed, the Attacker wins.
- Otherwise, the Defender wins.

## Command Points in Open Play

The Open Play rules page does not specify CP amounts or CP mechanics. Open Play does not use the Battle-Forged system or Strategic Reserves rules. Based on the broader 9th Edition rules context (see also `setup.md`): Open Play uses 3 CP per player as a fixed starting value, though this may come from a different section of the rulebook.

## Key Differences from Matched Play

| Aspect                  | Matched Play                        | Open Play                          |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Army selection          | Battle-Forged, point limits apply   | Any models, no restrictions        |
| Points / Power Level    | Points required                     | Power Level optional               |
| Starting CP             | 3 / 6 / 12 / 18 (by battle size)   | Not specified (app uses 3)         |
| Secondary Objectives    | 3 per player, category restricted   | None                               |
| VP maximum              | 100 (45+45+10)                      | Varies by mission                  |
| Missions                | 15–18 missions by battle size       | 3 missions                         |
| Battle Ready bonus      | +10 VP                              | Not mentioned                      |
| Attacker/Defender       | Roll-off                            | Higher Power Level = Attacker      |
| Deploy order            | Defender deploys first              | Attacker deploys first             |
| First Turn              | Roll-off                            | Roll-off (Defender decides in D/G) |
| Detachment rules        | Required (Battle-Forged)            | Not applicable                     |
| Strategic Reserves      | Rules apply (50% limit)             | Not mentioned                      |

## Notes and Observations

- Open Play explicitly states armies "need not be equal" — asymmetric battles are intended.
- The three missions scale for power imbalance: equal armies use Annihilation, unbalanced use Death or Glory where the weaker army defends.
- Power Level is a rough guide; the game does not enforce Power Level balancing.
- No painting bonus (Battle Ready) exists in Open Play rules.
- In Death or Glory, the Defender wins by simply surviving — this favors the smaller army.
