## Overview

Matched Play is the competitive format for Warhammer 40,000 9th Edition. It requires Battle-Forged armies, point limits, and uses the Matched Play mission packs (e.g., the Grand Tournament Mission Pack). It uses a 16-step pre-battle and battle sequence.

## The Matched Play Sequence (16 Steps)

### STEP 1 — SELECT BATTLE SIZE
Players agree on the battle size (Combat Patrol / Incursion / Strike Force / Onslaught). This determines points limit, starting CP, detachment limits, and available missions.

### STEP 2 — MUSTER ARMIES
Each player selects a Battle-Forged army within the points limit for the chosen battle size. Key restrictions:

- **No Understrength Units:** "You cannot include any Understrength units in your army."
- **Faction Keyword Rule:** All units in each Detachment must share at least one Faction keyword. The shared keyword cannot be CHAOS, IMPERIUM, AELDARI, YNNARI, or TYRANIDS (with exceptions for Fortification Networks).
- **Datasheet Duplicate Limit:** "Each player can only include the same datasheet in their army three times." Exceptions apply for Troops, Dedicated Transports, and Reinforcement units. Daemon Prince variants count as the same datasheet.
- **Aircraft Limits:**
  - Combat Patrol / Incursion: maximum 1 aircraft
  - Strike Force: maximum 2 aircraft
  - Onslaught: maximum 3 aircraft
- **Aircraft Deployment:** Aircraft must start in Strategic Reserves.
- **Combat Patrol Detachments:** Only one Patrol Detachment is allowed (or a Super-heavy Detachment for IMPERIAL KNIGHTS / CHAOS KNIGHTS armies).
- **Pre-Battle Stratagems (Upgrades):** "If either player has access to any Stratagems that are used before the battle that upgrade units, these must be used now and the details of the upgrades noted on the player's army roster." This is when Warlord Traits and Relics are assigned via Stratagems.

### STEP 3 — DETERMINE MISSION
Players either agree on a mission or roll randomly from the mission table for their battle size:

| Battle Size   | Available Missions                                                          |
|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Combat Patrol | Incisive Attack, Outriders, Encircle (3 missions)                          |
| Incursion     | Divide and Conquer through Shifting Front (6 missions)                      |
| Strike Force  | Retrieval Mission through Vital Intelligence (6 missions)                   |
| Onslaught     | Lines of Battle, All-out War, Pathway to Glory (3 missions)                |

### STEP 4 — READ MISSION BRIEFING
Players review the mission's primary objectives, secondary objective options (if mission-specific ones exist), and any special mission rules.

### STEP 5 — PLACE OBJECTIVE MARKERS
Players set up objective markers on the battlefield according to the mission's deployment map.

### STEP 6 — CREATE THE BATTLEFIELD
Players establish terrain features on a rectangular battlefield. Minimum battlefield sizes correspond to battle size:

- Combat Patrol / Incursion: 44" × 30" minimum
- Strike Force: 44" × 60" minimum
- Onslaught: 44" × 90" minimum

### STEP 7 — SELECT SECONDARY OBJECTIVES
"Each player then secretly selects three secondary objectives for the battle and writes them down." Players then simultaneously reveal their selections.

- **Category Restriction:** "When you select secondary objectives, you cannot choose more than one from each category."
- **Scoring Cap:** "You can score no more than 15 victory points from each secondary objective you select during the mission."
- **Secondary Objective Categories:**
  1. Purge the Enemy
  2. No Mercy, No Respite
  3. Battlefield Supremacy
  4. Shadow Operations
  5. Warpcraft

**Scoring Types:**
- *Progressive Objectives:* Scored during the battle at specified times (e.g., end of Command phase, end of battle round).
- *End Game Objectives:* Scored at the end of the battle only.

**Example Secondary (Battlefield Supremacy):**
- DOMINATION: "Score 3 victory points if you control more than half the total number of objective markers on the battlefield at the end of your turn."

**Example Secondary (No Mercy, No Respite):**
- RAISE THE BANNERS HIGH: "1 victory point at the end of each of your Command phases, and 1 victory point at the end of the battle."

### STEP 8 — DETERMINE ATTACKER AND DEFENDER
Players roll off; the winner decides which role (Attacker or Defender) they wish to take.

### STEP 9 — CHOOSE DEPLOYMENT ZONE
The Defender selects their deployment zone; the Attacker takes the remaining zone.

### STEP 10 — DECLARE RESERVES AND TRANSPORTS
Players secretly note which units will be placed in Strategic Reserves, alternate deployment locations, or embarked in TRANSPORT models, then simultaneously reveal their selections.

**Strategic Reserves Rules:**
- No more than half the total number of units in an army can be Strategic Reserve and/or Reinforcement units.
- The combined points value of all Strategic Reserve and Reinforcement units must be less than half of the army's total points value.
- Reserves cannot arrive in battle round one.
- Reserves count as destroyed if not arrived by end of battle round three.

### STEP 11 — DEPLOY ARMIES
Players alternate deploying units, starting with the Defender. Units must be set up wholly within their deployment zone. If one player finishes deploying first, the opponent deploys all remaining units. Units with post-deployment special abilities trigger after both armies are fully deployed; players roll off to determine order, starting with the winner.

### STEP 12 — DETERMINE FIRST TURN
Players roll off; the winner chooses whether to take the first or second turn. No CP compensation is given to the player going second.

### STEP 13 — RESOLVE PRE-BATTLE ABILITIES
"Players alternate resolving any pre-battle abilities units in their army may have, and resolving any Stratagems that are used before the battle (excluding those that upgrade their units or those that enable them to set up a unit in a location other than the battlefield)."

Note: Stratagems that upgrade units (Warlord Traits, Relics) are resolved in Step 2, not here.

### STEP 14 — BEGIN THE BATTLE
Battle rounds commence. Each battle round consists of both players taking a turn.

### STEP 15 — ENDING THE BATTLE
The battle ends when one army has no models remaining on the battlefield, or after the fifth battle round completes — whichever comes first.

### STEP 16 — DETERMINE VICTOR
"The player with the most victory points is the winner. If players are tied, the battle is a draw." No tiebreaker rules beyond total VP count.

## Victory Points System

### Primary Objectives
- Maximum: **45 victory points** from primary objectives.
- Described per mission in mission briefings.
- Can be progressive (scored during battle) or end-game.

### Secondary Objectives
- Maximum: **45 victory points** from secondaries (3 objectives × 15 VP cap each).
- Each player selects 3 secondaries from different categories.

### Battle Ready Bonus
- "If every model in a player's army was painted to a Battle Ready standard, that player is awarded a bonus 10 victory points."
- This is optional/honour-system; no pre-game agreement requirement mentioned.

### Total Possible VP
- 45 (primary) + 45 (secondary) + 10 (Battle Ready) = **100 VP maximum**

## Command Points (CP) Rules

- **Starting CP:** Determined by battle size (see table above).
- **Per-Round CP Gain:** The Matched Play page does not specify automatic per-round CP regeneration. CP gain during the game comes from specific secondary objectives or unit/Stratagem abilities.
- **CP and Battle-Forged:** Battle-Forged armies receive the starting CP for their battle size. Non-Battle-Forged rules are not addressed on this page (Matched Play requires Battle-Forged).

## Army Composition — Detachment Rules

### Battle-Forged Requirement
Matched Play requires Battle-Forged armies. All detachments must follow faction keyword rules.

### Detachment Limits by Battle Size
- **Combat Patrol:** Only one Patrol Detachment (exception: one Super-heavy Detachment for IMPERIAL/CHAOS KNIGHTS).
- **Incursion / Strike Force / Onslaught:** Multiple detachments allowed; all must share faction keywords.

Note: Specific detachment slot tables (HQ/Troops/Elites/etc. min-max per detachment type) are defined in the core Battle-Forged army rules, not on this Matched Play page.

### Fortification Network Detachment
Exists as a special detachment type with "no effect on your Army Faction." Used for fortification units; does not need to share faction keywords with the rest of the army.

## Notes and Observations

- The Matched Play page itself does not include per-round CP regeneration rules — these likely appear in the core Command Phase rules.
- Detachment slot tables are in core Battle-Forged rules, not on this page.
- No "going second" CP bonus exists in 9th Edition Matched Play.
- Reserves arriving late (after round 3) count as destroyed — important for VP calculations.
